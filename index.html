Postal Code Systems Around the World

India – PIN Codes

India uses a 6-digit Postal Index Number (PIN) system where each digit represents a specific region, sub-region, and delivery post office.

United States – ZIP Codes

US ZIP codes consist of five digits, with optional ZIP+4 extensions for identifying specific delivery routes or buildings.

United Kingdom – Postcodes

UK postcodes are alphanumeric and provide highly precise address identification, often down to a small group of houses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a postal code?

A postal code is a numeric or alphanumeric identifier used by postal services to organize and deliver mail efficiently.

Can I search postal codes by city or area?
